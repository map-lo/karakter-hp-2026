## ABOUT

KARAKTER is an artist management company based in Berlin and London, established in 2005 by Peter Putz, Liz Riches, and Maximilian Schenkel.

Karakter's artists have released albums that have won, or been nominated for, awards including Grammys, Tonys, Meteors, the Choice Music Prize, BBC Folk Awards, Echo, Deutscher Musikautorenpreis, Preis fuer Popkultur and Amadeus Awards among others. Their music has been used in movies, advertising campaigns and TV shows. They have written score music, created Broadway shows, and played thousands of live shows and festivals worldwide.

## Team

The team has grown over the years to include additional staff members who align with Karakter's values and approach. The company has also expanded to include sister companies Love Your Artist (ticketing and merch), Spokes (live promoter and booking agency, specialised in comedy, podcasts and spoken word) and Heavy German Shit (management agency representing artist and creators across comedy, podcasts, TV and literature).
