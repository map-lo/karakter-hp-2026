description: "Privacy policy of Karakter Worldwide GmbH."
---

Karakter Management collects personal information when you subscribe to their newsletter. Typically, this comprises only your email address. For registrations prior to October 8th, 2016, they may retain your name, country, town, and/or postal code if provided.

This website is hosted on the Squarespace platform and your information is stored on secure servers behind a firewall.

Information is gathered exclusively for newsletter communications. The organization states they do not share subscriber details with external parties.

Subscribers can terminate their newsletter membership using the unsubscribe link included at the bottom of each message.

You may request to access your stored data, correct incomplete or inaccurate information, or request deletion of your personal data by contacting the organization via email. Karakter Management may ask for identifying information before processing access requests to ensure legitimate requests.

This privacy policy only applies to Karakter Worldwide. External websites linked from their site operate under separate policies.

The site is not designed for minors, and they do not intentionally gather information about children.
